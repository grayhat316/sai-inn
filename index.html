Welcome to Sai Inn

The jewel of the City of Champions

Sai Inn sits just off the Nairobi to Eldoret highway, a few minutes from Eldoret International Airport and a short drive from the Kerio Valley and Chepkit Falls.

Our rooms are modern and give you room to stretch out. Outside, the solarium terrace courtyard is the place for coffee from the restaurant, free Wi-Fi and a slow afternoon.

Everything we cook starts from our own farm produce. It is part of our Healthy Living Policy, so you can relax knowing the food is organic and fresh.

Whether you are here for a match, a conference or a quiet weekend, you get the same welcome: a cold drink, a hot shower and a team that knows your name by the second day.

  • Free Wi-Fi throughout
  • On-site restaurant
  • Solarium terrace courtyard
  • Fresh food from our farm
  • 24/7 reception
  • Minutes from the airport
